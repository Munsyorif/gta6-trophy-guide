Roadmap to the platinum Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies

1 Play the story 60–80 h

Finish all 5 chapters. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — the one possibly missable trophy. Keep a manual save at each chapter start: no mission replay, no New Game+.

Covers: all 17 story trophies, side-character finales, first activities.

2 100% checklist ~100 h

After the credits it opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — lenient, about 60% of the game. Do 70 of 80 challenges, scan 200 of 277 vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals. Rob all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations.

Covers: A Long Road, stunts, ranges, races, collectibles, vaults.

3 Cleanup ~10 h

Stranger strands, partner trophies, one-offs (cocktail, showroom, bowling alley), max strength for both, 500 Criminal Profile actions, money trophies. Then the platinum pops.

Covers: the remaining activities and the platinum itself.

Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy save. Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.
